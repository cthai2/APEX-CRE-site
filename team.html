Leadership Team

Our vertically integrated team brings deep expertise in acquisitions, rigorous underwriting, and operational execution.

APEX CRE, backed by the institutional strength of Massive Capital, is built on delivering solid returns without speculation. By partnering with passive investors and local operators, our team leverages deep financial and management expertise to act as a true CFO for our partners.

We co-invest side-by-side in every deal to ensure our interests are absolutely aligned with yours. Furthermore, we empower local operators to acquire their next asset through strategic equity, debt, and asset management support, effectively mitigating local operational risk while maximizing investor returns across multiple markets.

Our Mission

Simplify real estate investing with the purpose of enhancing the lives of our partners & colleagues while impacting local communities.

Our Vision

We enable Passive Investors to create wealth by purchasing ownership of income producing real estate properties across multiple markets.
