Frequently Asked Questions

APEX CRE is a private Real Estate Investment Company actively investing (owning, operating, and funding) in multiple asset classes (Land, Industrial, Multifamily) across core markets. Much like crowdfunding platforms, APEX CRE enables Accredited and Sophisticated investors to come together to invest in a common project. With APEX CRE, investors are able to:

  • Partner with full-time real estate investors.
  • De-risk portfolio by investing across asset classes and multiple markets.
  • Invest in multifamily/industrial real estate projects seamlessly through our online portal.

APEX CRE works with both accredited and sophisticated investors.

The SEC defines an accredited investor as anyone who:

  • Earned income that exceeded $200,000 (or $300,000 together with a spouse or spousal equivalent) in each of the prior two years, and reasonably expects the same for the current year, OR
  • Has a net worth over $1 million, either alone or together with a spouse or spousal equivalent (excluding the value of the person’s primary residence), OR
  • Holds in good standing a Series 7, 65, or 82 license.

The commission defines a sophisticated investor as an individual or institution that "must have sufficient knowledge and experience in financial and business matters to make them capable of evaluating the merits and risks of the prospective investment."

The minimum investment is typically around $75,000.

Investing with an IRA is a popular way to invest with APEX CRE. We make investing with your IRA a seamless process and we have worked with dozens of IRA custodians. Connect with us for more details.

APEX CRE purchases Land, Industrial, and Multifamily real estate. Typical Multifamily assets have at least 50 units or more. Typical Industrial is between 10,000-30,000 SQFT leasable space. Typical Land is a 5-200 acre tract for new development. Details for each investment opportunity are shared as they become available.

APEX CRE always invests side-by-side with our investors. We make money from the real estate we own with our investors, which includes rental income & profits from the sale of properties.

Each real estate investment opportunity is different. Depending on the asset class, APEX CRE typically aims to deliver our partners double-digit overall returns:

  • Multifamily: We target in excess of 10% per year of overall return.
  • Land: We target in excess of 15% per year.
  • Industrial (Cash Flow): We target in excess of 8% per year of cash flow.

All of our investment opportunities include financial projections prepared by APEX CRE, based upon our expertise and educated assumptions. While we like to provide conservative projections, it is required that each investor understands that past performance does not indicate nor guarantee future results.

In the commercial real estate investment context, risk is anything that creates volatility in a property or portfolio’s expected or actual returns. Learn more about risks on our Investment Disclaimer page.

You are always welcome to visit the property that you have invested in in person. We also make all the details for each property available online through your portal for review.

APEX CRE distributes earnings to our partners on a quarterly basis.
