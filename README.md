# APEX CRE Partners — Commercial Real Estate Syndication & Investment Platform

## Purpose of the Project
The primary purpose of the **APEX CRE** web platform is to serve as a digital deal-room and investor relations hub for commercial real estate syndication. Designed for General Partners (GPs) and underwriters, the platform bridges the gap between sponsors and accredited or sophisticated investors—showcasing institutional-grade value-add assets (such as Multi-family, Industrial, Land, and RV parks) while streamlining underwriting metrics, legal compliance disclosures, and investor onboarding.

## Key Features & Architecture
* **Interactive Portfolio (`portfolio.html`):** Dynamically renders active investment offerings and property metrics (including IRR targets, Equity Multiples, and Cash-on-Cash yields) to help partners evaluate deal performance.
* **Acquisitions & Process (`acquisitions.html`):** Highlights a structured 5-step acquisition timeline (Sourcing, Analysis, Due Diligence, Execution, Value Creation) alongside explicit property criteria to attract brokers and deal flow.
* **Interactive Investor FAQ (`faq.html`):** Features responsive accordion dropdowns covering investor qualifications (Accredited vs. Sophisticated definitions), minimum capital thresholds ($75,000), IRA custodial integrations, and distribution schedules.
* **Legal & Compliance Infrastructure:** Dedicated, production-ready pages for **Privacy Policy**, **Terms and Conditions**, and **Investment Disclaimer** (covering Regulation D exemptions, forward-looking statements, and risk disclosures).
* **Responsive Brand Identity:** Built using a high-contrast dark navy palette (`#0d1b2a`) and gold accents (`#d4af37`), featuring fixed watermark iconography, responsive mobile navigation, and a 5-second "Under Construction" redirect utility for restricted portals.

## Tech Stack
