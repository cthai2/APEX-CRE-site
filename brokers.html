Our Multi-Asset Buy-Box Criteria

Apex CRE Partners actively acts as principal buyers across multiple commercial asset classes. Below are our specific underwriting parameters and acquisition targets.

RV

Commercial RV Parks

Size: 50+ Pad Sites
Location: Sunbelt region, high-growth transit & recreation corridors.
Profile: Value-add, under-market daily/monthly rates, or expansion acreage.

SS

Self-Storage Facilities

Size: 30,+ NRSF (Net Rentable Square Feet) or expansion potential.
Location: Growing MSAs with strong population density.
Profile: Climate/non-climate mix, low economic occupancy, or conversion upside.

MF

Multifamily Apartments

Size: 40 to 150+ Units
Location: B/C class submarkets with strong job growth.
Profile: Operational inefficiencies, deferred maintenance, or light-to-heavy value-add renovation potential.

RAL

Residential Assisted Living (RAL)

Size: 6 to 16+ Bed Capacity Homes
Location: Affluent residential suburban corridors.
Profile: Licensed operational facilities or residential properties adaptable for senior care use.

NNN

Triple Net Lease (NNN) Retail

Size: $1M to $10M Transaction Value
Location: High-traffic retail nodes and pad sites.
Profile: Single or multi-tenant corporate/national credit leases with stable cash flow.

MHP

Mobile Home Parks (MHP)

Size: 30+ Pads
Location: Municipal utilities preferred (city water/sewer).
Profile: Park-owned home (POH) reduction upside, infill vacancy fill, or sub-metering opportunities.

FLX

Flex Space

Size: 15,000 to 60,000 SQFT
Location: Industrial/commercial business parks.
Profile: Multi-tenant configurations catering to local service providers and contractors.

WH

Warehouse & Distribution

Size: 20,000 to 100,000 SQFT
Location: Proximity to major freight corridors and logistics hubs.
Profile: Clear heights 18ft+, dock-high doors, or single-to-multi tenant conversion angles.
